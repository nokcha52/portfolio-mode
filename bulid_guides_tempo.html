Introduction

The Mode Tempo is a premium compact 60% mechanical keyboard featuring an engineered stanced visual profile. Please follow the instructions below step-by-step to align with your downloaded reference images and ensure clean layout installation.

Join the Mode Community!

If you have any questions before or during your build, join our Discord server to connect with fellow builders and get real-time tips. Once you finish your masterpiece, tag us at @modekeyboards on Instagram—we love seeing your builds!

What's inside the box?

Clear your workspace on a plush micro-fiber mat and organize the following pieces:

Hardware Components:

  • 1x Tempo Chassis (Top Case & Bottom Frame)
  • 1x Tempo Accent Weight (with integrated daughterboard seating)
  • 1x Custom PCB & Daughterboard (with JST cable)
  • 1x Plate (if selected)
  • 1x Fastener Pack (Assorted M2 screws and an H1.5 / H2.0 hex key)
  • 1x Poron Dampening Foam Kit (Plate & Case Foam)
  • 1x Silicone Feet Set & Force Break Pads

Mounting Styles

The Tempo offers two entirely different mounting choices that dramatically alter the stiffness and sound pitch. Pick your favorite route before configuring the internal assembly:

Isolated Top Mount (Crisp & Energetic):

Slide your chosen mounting blocks onto the plate tabs. This creates a highly uniform, slightly firmer typing deck with clear, bright acoustic feedback.

Top Mount (Dampened & Solid):

Insert the top mount blocks into the slots inside the top case, align with the plate, and secure them to the top case using screws.

Step-by-step Assembly

[01] Test the PCB

Open your web browser and navigate to the online VIA testing suite. Toggle on the 'Key Tester' layout.

01. Launch VIA Configurator

Open your web browser and navigate to the online VIA testing suite. Toggle on the 'Key Tester' layout. Plug the daughterboard JST wire into the main PCB, then connect it to your computer via USB-C. Take a pair of metal tweezers and touch both copper contact leaves on every hot-swap socket sequentially. Ensure the grid boxes on your monitor light up

02. Run the Matrix Check

Take a pair of metal tweezers and touch both copper contact leaves on every hot-swap socket sequentially to verify they light up before proceeding.

Pro tip: Dead Socket?

If a specific key fails to trigger an on-screen reaction, check if the hot-swap metal leaf on the back of the PCB is accidentally bent wide open. Reach out to support@modedesigns.com

01. Secure Lower Feet & Weight

Press the small round exterior silicone feet into their designated corner slots on the bottom frame. Next, lower the large accent weight into the chassis housing and firmly press the long custom silicone bar feet straight into the horizontal weight slots.

Customization & Software

Configuring layouts on the Sonnet requires zero proprietary download packages.

01. Instant Key Remapping

Plug the board in and navigate to the VIA web application. Any macro, layer assignment, or media key adjustment changes instantly on the keyboard hardware memory.

02. Entering Bootloader Mode

If you need to flash custom QMK firmware, hold Fn + Esc while plugging in the cable, or press the physical reset button located on the bottom of the PCB.

Tip for Mac Users:

When using the keyboard on macOS, you can swap the Command and Option key mapping natively under System Preferences -> Keyboard to keep your layout seamless and intuitive across different operating systems.
