About Mode

Designed with Intention. Built for Enthusiasts.

We believe the objects
you touch every day
should be a source of constant joy.

Driven by Curiosity,
Perfected by Obsession.

We looked at the ordinary keyboard and asked, “Why can’t this everyday tool be an inspiring piece of object?” We don’t just build input devices; we create a sensory experience. From the resonance of the typing sound to the heavy, clean silhouette on your desk, every detail is engineered to turn a mundane workspace into a place of beautiful inspiration.

Tactile Emotion

The Friction, the Texture, the Sound. Like the gentle friction of a premium pen on paper or the crisp texture of turning a book page, we obsess over the analog, tactile feedback of our keyboards. Beauty plays a role in utility.

02
Substance Over Bulk

Make Things That Deserve to Exist. We refuse to create mass-produced junk or take shortcuts. We design minimal, high-detail objects with true substance, ensuring every single item sold on our site carries absolute purpose and value.

Behind the Obsession

Our commitment to thoughtful design doesn't end with the product; it begins with the unboxing. We have completely overmatched our identity system to introduce 100% circular, eco-friendly packaging. Moving entirely away from synthetic foam inserts, our upcoming runs utilize premium molded pulp derived from unbleached organic materials—ensuring that our footprint on the planet is as clean as our design aesthetic.
