Introduction

This guide will walk you through assembling and getting started with your new Mode Encore keyboard. Please read through each step carefully and avoid skipping ahead to ensure the best possible build experience and acoustics.

Join the Mode Community!

If you have any questions before or during your build, join our Discord server to connect with fellow builders and get real-time tips. Once you finish your masterpiece, tag us at @modekeyboards on Instagram—we love seeing your builds!

What's inside the box?

Before you start, make sure you have all the necessary components. (Estimated build time: 45–90 minutes)

Included Components:

  • 1x Encore Case (Pre-assembled Top and Bottom case)
  • 1x PCB & Daughterboard (with JST cable)
  • 1x Plate
  • 1x Mounting Block Set
  • 1x Screwdriver & Fastener Pack (Screws)
  • 4x Silicone Feet
  • 1x Keycap & Switch Puller
  • 1x Stabilizer Set

Required (Not Included):

  • Switches (x70) and Keycaps
  • (Optional) Foam Kit
  • (Optional) Stabilizer Lubricant

Mounting Styles

The Mode Encore features a highly versatile internal mounting system designed to cater to your specific typing preference. Choose your preferred mounting style before starting the assembly:

Lattice Block Mount

Offers a flexible, bouncy, and soft typing experience.

  • Green Lattice Blocks: Highest flex (Most Flex)
  • Black Lattice Blocks: Balanced flexibility (Mid Flex)
  • Green Half-Lattice Blocks: Firm yet forgiving (Low Flex)

Solid Block Mount

Provides a responsive, crisp, yet highly comfortable typing experience.

Isolated Top Mount

Delivers a lively responsiveness paired with a generally lower-pitched acoustic profile.

Step-by-step Assembly

[01] Test the PCB

Testing your PCB before final housing assembly is highly recommended to ensure everything is functioning correctly.

01. Prepare the Software:

Open the VIA web configurator, navigate to the 'Key Tester' tab, and activate the 'Test Matrix' mode.

02. Connect and Test

Place the PCB face down on a non-conductive surface, connect the daughterboard, and plug in the USB-C cable. Use metal tweezers to touch the two hot-swap socket contacts for each key, checking if the corresponding key lights up in VIA.

Pro tip: Encountered an issue?

If a specific key fails to register during testing, pause your assembly and contact our support team immediately at support@modedesigns.com

Customization & Software

The Mode Encore is designed to be fully customizable directly through your web browser without requiring bloated software.

01. VIA / QMK Compatibility

Map your keys, configure layers, and manage macros easily using the web-based VIA tool

  • step 1
  • step 2

02. Entering Bootloader Mode

If you need to flash custom QMK firmware, hold Fn + Esc while plugging in the cable, or press the physical reset button located on the bottom of the PCB.

Tip for Mac Users:

When using the keyboard on macOS, you can swap the Command and Option key mapping natively under System Preferences -> Keyboard to keep your layout seamless and intuitive across different operating systems.
