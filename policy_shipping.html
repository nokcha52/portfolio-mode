Shipping & Delivery policy

Overview

At Mode, we strive to deliver every order as efficiently and reliably as possible. Shipping times may vary depending on product availability, order volume, customization requirements, and destination. Once an order has been processed and shipped, customers will receive a confirmation email containing tracking information.

Please note that all delivery estimates are provided as guidelines and are not guaranteed.

Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1-5 business days after payment has been confirmed.
Processing times may be longer during:

  • Product launches
  • Restocks and pre-order events
  • Promotional periods
  • Holidays and peak shipping seasons

Orders containing multiple items may be held until all items are available for shipment.

Domestic Shipping

Domestic orders are generally delivered within 3-7 business days after shipment. Delivery times may vary depending on:

  • Customer location
  • Carrier service levels
  • Seasonal shipping volume
  • Weather or transportation disruptions

Customers will receive tracking information once their order leaves our fulfillment facility.

International Shipping

Mode ships to many regions worldwide.
International delivery estimates vary by destination and carrier but generally range between:

7-21 Business Days

after shipment.
Delivery times may be affected by:

  • Customs clearance procedures
  • Local im port regulations
  • National holidays
  • Carrier delays

International customers are responsible for verifying import requirements applicable to their country before placing an order.

Duties & Taxes

International orders may be subject to:

  • Customs duties
  • Import taxes
  • VAT/GST
  • Brokerage fees

These charges are determined by local authorities and are not included in the product or shipping price unless otherwise specified.

Mode is not responsible for any additional fees incurred during importation.

Tracking Information

Once your order has been shipped, a tracking number will be sent to the email address associated with your order.

Tracking information may take up to 48 hours to become active after shipment confirmation.
Customers are encouraged to monitor tracking updates directly through the carrier's website.

Shipping Delays

While we work closely with our shipping partners, delivery times may occasionally be affected by circumstances beyond our control.
Examples include:

  • Severe weather conditions
  • Customs inspections
  • Carrier operational disruptions
  • Regional transportation issues
  • High-volume holiday periods

Mode is not responsible for delays caused by third-party shipping providers after a package has been handed over for delivery.

Lost Packages

If your package appears lost in transit, please contact our support team as soon as possible.
To assist with an investigation, we may request:

  • Order number
  • Shipping address
  • Tracking information
  • Additional documentation if necessary

We will work with the carrier to determine the status of the shipment and identify the most appropriate resolution.

Incorrect Shipping Information

Customers are responsible for ensuring that shipping information is accurate at the time of purchase.
If an incorrect address is provided:

  • Additional shipping fees may apply
  • Delivery delays may occur
  • Orders may be returned to sender

Mode cannot guarantee modifications to shipping information once fulfillment has begun.

Refused Deliveries

If a shipment is refused by the recipient or returned due to unpaid customs fees, import duties, or unsuccessful delivery attempts:

  • Original shipping charges are non-refundable
  • Return shipping fees may be deducted from any approved refund
  • Additional handling fees may apply

Multiple Shipments

In some cases, orders containing multiple products may be shipped separately.
This may occur when:

  • Products become available at different times
  • Items ship from different fulfillment locations
  • Pre-order and in-stock products are combined in the same order

Customers will receive tracking information for each shipment when applicable.

Need Assistance?

If you have questions regarding cancellations, returns, refunds, or warranty coverage, please contact our support team.
