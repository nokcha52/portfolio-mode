How We Design and Make Keyboards:
The Pursuit of Joy and Tactile Artistry.

A look inside the Mode philosophy—where digital workflows meet analog warmth, and engineering becomes a personal canvas.

Author : Matthew Encina

Designing Joy:
A Look Inside Mode Designs

Most mechanical keyboards on the market are born from a sterile technical requirement: the need to input characters into a machine. They are often treated as mere commodity goods, mass-produced and stripped of character. But at Mode Designs, we look at that blank aluminum rectangle on your desk and see something entirely different. We see an object that has no business being this boring. We see a medium for joy.

Our journey into keyboard design began not as a commercial blueprint, but as a genuine, healthy obsession that spiraled beautifully out of control. To truly understand how we design and make our keyboards, you must look beyond the CNC milling machines and the aluminum anodizing tanks. You have to understand our foundational philosophy: "Warm Tech."

From Code to Craft
The Birth of an Obsession

Mode was founded by Jacob, a former software engineer who spent the formative years of his career staring at screens and typing on standard plastic peripherals. Like many professionals tethered to a digital landscape, he felt a lingering jealousy toward artisans who worked with their hands—photographers adjusting mechanical lenses, chefs choosing tactile tools, and woodworkers selecting grain.

When Jacob discovered the custom mechanical keyboard hobby, it provided an immediate connection between his digital career and the physical world. It was a nerd-centric rabbit hole where engineering, physical materials, and daily workflow met. It became a reward for hard work—a tool that transformed a mundane task into a deeply sensory ritual.

This balance between the digital and the analog became the cornerstone of Mode. We realized that our environments deeply impact our daily lives. When the objects around us are beautiful, intentional, and rich in tactile feedback, our daily interactions become fundamentally more joyful.

Embracing the Analog
The Importance of Tactility

In an increasingly digital era where everything is smoothed over by touchscreens and cloud software, humanity is experiencing a quiet craving for analog feedback. We find joy in the slight friction of a fountain pen scratching across textured paper, the physical resistance of turning a page in a well-bound book, or the solid mechanical clack of a well-tuned keyboard.

Our design process always begins by honoring this tactile relationship. When we design a keyboard like the Sonnet or the Encore, we don't just engineer for the eyes; we engineer for the fingertips and the ears.

This tactile philosophy is why we brought Matthew Encina on board as our Chief Design Officer (CDO). With over 20 years of experience as a creative director, graphic designer, and educator, Matthew’s approach to keyboard design is heavily influenced by mediums outside traditional tech—specifically interior architecture, premium fashion, industrial design, and fine furniture.

The Art of the Mix
Colors, Materials, and Curation

How do we translate this "Warm Tech" philosophy into physical manufacturing? We do it by mastering the art of mixed materials.

Before Mode, the keyboard world rarely experimented with organic, premium textures in a cohesive way. We broke that mold by introducing high-end material pairings that you would typically find in a mid-century modern living room or a luxury watch boutique:

  • Natural Hardwoods: Incorporating accents like White Oak and Walnut to ground the cold metal chassis with organic warmth.
  • Polished Heavy Metals: Utilizing solid brass, copper, and mirror-finished steel to add mass, premium weight, and deep acoustic resonance.
  • Curated Palettes: Designing complex colorways—such as Black Sesame, Forest Green, Mocha, and Studio Light—that draw inspiration from high fashion and interior decor rather than standard gaming aesthetics.

Every detail is meticulously curated. We don't just look at the color of the anodized top case; we scrutinize the finish of the fasteners, the geometry of the rubber feet, the texture of the included carrying bags, and how a specific plate material (like POM, Carbon Fiber, or Copper) interacts with the internal acoustic chain. It is an extensive process of prototyping, testing, and refining until the board feels less like a gadget and more like a piece of bespoke desktop architecture.

At its core, a product cannot bring true joy if the culture surrounding it is cold and exclusionary. The custom keyboard space can often feel intimidating, technical, and gatekept. From day one, Mode has set out to redefine not just how keyboards are made, but how the community is treated.

We believe that premium engineering must be matched by an equally empathetic, human-centric customer experience. Our teams interact directly with our community on Discord, listen to feedback, and show up at mechanical keyboard meetups to look at the real faces behind the digital usernames. Whether we are troubleshooting a custom layout, accommodating a last-minute address change, or resolving a fulfillment delay, we approach our customers with absolute transparency and respect. We showcase this community spirit through our monthly build updates and collaborative spotlights, celebrating how enthusiasts pair our boards with custom switch selections (like Alexotos Obscura Linears), unique plate options, and beautiful keycap sets (like GMK Cosmos or GMK Analog Dreams).

The Ultimate Goal:
A Blank Canvas for Your Story

When you receive a Mode keyboard, you are not just buying a tool that we built. You are receiving a curated canvas that we perfected, waiting for your personal story to finish it. You can assemble it once with your favorite switches and keycaps and leave it as a permanent monument on your desk, or you can completely tear it down and rebuild it fifty times to explore new acoustic and tactile landscapes.

We design and make keyboards because we believe that the things you touch every single day should be an absolute joy to see, hear, feel, and experience. We do the heavy engineering so that your personal curation can do the talking.

Questions

Everything You Need to Know

"Warm Tech" is our guiding principle that refines how technology fits into our daily lives. Instead of designing cold, sterile, and purely utilitarian gadgets, we create objects that possess a distinct personality, bring organic warmth to your living space, and provide a deeply satisfying tactile experience. By blending traditional tech engineering with inspirations from premium furniture, fashion, and interior design, we make keyboards that feel like a seamless extension of your home or studio.

Our design process relies heavily on the art of mixed materials. We look beyond standard aluminum to find textures and elements that evoke an emotional and sensory response. This includes using sustainably sourced natural hardwoods (like White Oak and Walnut) for organic contrast, and heavy, high-density metals (like solid brass, copper, and mirror-finished stainless steel) to give the chassis substantial structural weight and a premium acoustic backdrop.

As professionals who spend hours in front of screens, we believe that typing shouldn’t just be a mechanical input—it should be a joyful daily ritual. Human beings naturally crave analog feedback in a digital world. We meticulously tune the internal architecture, plate flex patterns, and gasket systems of every keyboard to isolate vibrations and eliminate harsh pings. This creates a clean, intentional acoustic profile that allows the unique personality of your chosen switches and plates to truly shine.

We offer both convenient hotswap PCBs (which allow you to plug in switches without soldering) and versatile solderable PCBs for veteran builders. Our out-of-the-box compatibility with open-source firmware like VIA/VIAL means you can easily customize your layouts and macros right through a web browser with zero technical barriers.

Our community is the heartbeat of our brand. Mode was born out of a genuine passion for the hobby, and we maintain that grassroots connection through radical transparency and empathy. Feedback from our Discord server and global mechanical keyboard meetups directly influences our R&D. Whether we are tweaking an acoustic buffer or designing a new colorway, we build our products alongside the community, ensuring our support and customer service remain human-centric, responsive, and welcoming to everyone.
