Introduction

The Mode Sonnet is a premium 75% mechanical keyboard designed to look elegant and offer maximum customizability. This guide covers the complete hardware assembly process. Please follow the instructions linearly to achieve the ideal sound signature and avoid damage to internal elements.

Join the Mode Community!

If you have any questions before or during your build, join our Discord server to connect with fellow builders and get real-time tips. Once you finish your masterpiece, tag us at @modekeyboards on Instagram—we love seeing your builds!

What's inside the box?

Before you unscrew anything, clear a soft workspace and ensure your packaging contains the following parts:

Included Components:

  • 1x Sonnet Chassis (Top Case, Bottom Case, and Accent piece pre-assembled)
  • 1x Internal Weight (Brass, Copper, Aluminum, or Steel)
  • 1x Custom PCB & Daughterboard (with JST cable)
  • 1x Plate
  • 1x Fastener Pack (Assorted M2 screws and an H1.5 / H2.0 hex key)
  • 1x Poron Dampening Foam Kit (Plate & Case Foam)
  • 1x Silicone Feet Set & Force Break Pads

Required (Not Included):

  • 1x Stabilizer Set (Screw-in type recommended)
  • 78x Mechanical Switches & Keycap Set

Mounting Styles

The Sonnet offers two entirely different mounting choices that dramatically alter the stiffness and sound pitch. Pick your favorite route before configuring the internal assembly:

Isolated Top Mount (Crisp & Energetic):

The plate is screwed directly to the top housing tabs using small silicone grommets. This creates a highly uniform, slightly firmer typing deck with clear, bright acoustic feedback.

Stack Mount (Dampened & Solid):

The plate/PCB combo sits snugly between a thick custom silicone or Poron case base foam piece and the top housing. This entirely eliminates internal hollow space, generating a deeper, highly dampened sound profile.

Step-by-step Assembly

[01] Test the Core PCB

Always test your PCB before screwing it inside the aluminum body. It saves hours of troubleshooting down the road.

01. Launch VIA Configurator

Open your web browser and navigate to the online VIA testing suite. Toggle on the 'Key Tester' layout.

Pro tip: Dead Socket?

If a specific key fails to trigger an on-screen reaction, check if the hot-swap metal leaf on the back of the PCB is accidentally bent wide open. Reach out to support@modedesigns.com

[02] Install Feet

01. Secure the Grip

Smoothly press and install the non-slip exterior silicone strip feet directly into the long recessed alignment channels on the bottom case.

[03] Detach Bottom Case

01. Enclosure Breakdown

Turn the pre-assembled case upside down on a micro-fiber towel. Use your H2.0 hex driver to remove the case fasteners and carefully separate the bottom case from the top case frame

  • step 1
  • step 2

02. Force Break Application

Stick the mini square silicone tape pieces directly around the chassis screw perimeter holes on the bottom case. This stops metallic pinging noises.

[04] Attach Daughterboard to Bottom Case

01. Fix the Daughterboard

Disconnect the temporary daughterboard cable from the main PCB. Keeping the cable connected tightly to the daughterboard end, lower it into the rear cutout of the bottom chassis. Secure it firmly using four M2x3 fasteners with your H1.5 driver

  • step 1
  • step 2

[05] Install PE Foam Sheet (Optional) & Stabilizers

01. Add Stabilizers

Lubricate and mount your screw-in stabilizers directly to the PCB. Make sure the small washers are placed between the screws and the PCB circuit track to avoid an electrical short.

02. Layer the Foam

Lay down your custom Sonnet Poron or PE plate foam sheet directly over the front of the PCB, ensuring it settles completely flat around the newly mounted stabilizers.

[06] Install Plate & Switches

01. Secure Plate to PCB

Align your plate over the dampening foam layer and snap 4-5 switches into the extreme corner locations and spacebar cluster. This aligns the plate and PCB perfectly.

  • step 1
  • step 2
  • step 3

02. Seat the Remaining Switches

Populate the rest of the layout with your switches. Crucial: Support the underside of the hot-swap sockets with your thumb while pressing each switch in from above to keep from bursting the solder pads off the board. Check all pins afterwards.

[07] Install Plate Caps & Accent Piece

01. Prep Plate Caps

Slide the soft silicone plate caps (or dampening rings) over the perimeter tabs around your plate.

[08] Weight Integration & Internal Configuration

01. [Route A] For Top Mount Configuration

Lower the internal weight into the recess of the bottom case and secure it with the shorter M3x5 flat head fasteners. Place the top case facedown, drop the plate/PCB assembly inside, and secure it via the tabs using M2x5 fasteners.

Lay the thick molded silicone or Poron base wedge cleanly on top, aligning it with the screw holes, and secure them both using the longer M3x7 flat head fasteners. Place the top case facedown and gently lower the plate/PCB assembly inside.

01. Connect and Seal

Hold the bottom case firmly in one hand and align it with the notch on the bottom edge of the top case to attach the daughterboard JST connector safely. Lower the top housing over the bottom layout. Fasten the bottom case back to the top case with the main M2.5x12 fasteners using your H2.0 driver until they are perfectly flush.

Customization & Software

The Mode Encore is designed to be fully customizable directly through your web browser without requiring bloated software.

01. Instant Key Remapping

Plug the board in and navigate to the VIA web application. Any macro, layer assignment, or media key adjustment changes instantly on the keyboard hardware memory.

02. Entering DFU Flash Mode

If you ever need to perform a hard firmware flash using QMK Toolbox, simply hold down Escape while inserting the USB cable. Alternatively, press the tiny tactile button on the underside of the PCB through the bottom case alignment hole.

  • step 1
  • step 2

Tip for Mac Users:

When using the keyboard on macOS, you can swap the Command and Option key mapping natively under System Preferences -> Keyboard to keep your layout seamless and intuitive across different operating systems.
